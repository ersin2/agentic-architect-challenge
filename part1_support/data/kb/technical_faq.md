# AcmeSync Technical FAQ

## Sync is slow or stuck
Sync can be slow on mobile networks or when battery saver is on. Open the app, go to Settings > Sync and tap Sync now. On Android, allow AcmeSync to run in the background in the system battery settings.

## Export to PDF fails
Export to PDF can fail for notebooks with more than 500 pages or very large images. Split the notebook into smaller notebooks, or export from the desktop app, which handles large files better. Export error 12 means the export timed out.

## App crashes when exporting
If the app crashes during export, update to the latest version from Settings > About > Check for updates. Large notebooks export more reliably from the desktop app.

## Resetting your password
Use the Forgot password link on the sign-in page. The reset link in the email is valid for 1 hour.

## Supported platforms
AcmeSync runs on Windows 10 and later, macOS 13 and later, Android 11 and later, iOS 16 and later, and in current versions of Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari.
