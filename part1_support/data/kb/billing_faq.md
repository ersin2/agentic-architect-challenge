# AcmeSync Billing FAQ

## Invoices
Invoices are emailed to the account owner after each payment. You can download any past invoice as a PDF from Settings > Billing > Invoice history.

## Payment methods
We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express and PayPal. You can update your card in Settings > Billing > Payment method.

## Plans and prices
AcmeSync has three plans. Personal costs $6 per month or $60 per year. Team costs $12 per user per month or $120 per user per year. Enterprise has custom pricing.

## Cancelling a subscription
You can cancel at any time in Settings > Billing > Cancel subscription. Cancelling stops the next renewal.

## Duplicate charges
If you see two charges for the same invoice, reply with the invoice number. Our billing team checks duplicate charges within 2 business days.

## Changing plans
Upgrades take effect immediately. Downgrades take effect at the next renewal date.
