# AcmeSync Refund Policy

Version 2026-09. This document is the only source of truth for refunds. Each clause has an id (R1 to R6).
Support replies must quote these clauses exactly and must not paraphrase them.

## R1 Annual plans
Annual plans can be refunded in full within 30 days of the original purchase date.

## R2 Monthly plans
Monthly plans are not refundable. Cancelling stops the next renewal, and access continues until the end of the paid month.

## R3 Refund timing
Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method within 5 to 10 business days.

## R4 Duplicate charges
If you were charged twice for the same invoice, the duplicate charge is refunded in full after our billing team confirms it.

## R5 Exclusions
Refunds are not available for accounts closed for violating the Terms of Service.

## R6 How to request a refund
Refund requests must come from the account owner's email address and must include the invoice number.
