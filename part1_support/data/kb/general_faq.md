# AcmeSync General FAQ

## Feedback and feature requests
We read every piece of feedback and every feature request, and we share them with the product team. We cannot promise if or when a requested feature will be built.

## Keyboard shortcuts
You can see all keyboard shortcuts in the desktop app under Help > Keyboard shortcuts.

## Response times
The support team answers emails within one business day. Urgent incidents are handled by the on-call team around the clock.
