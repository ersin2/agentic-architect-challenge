# AcmeSync Travel and Expense Policy

Version 3.2, effective 1 March 2026. This policy applies to all AcmeSync employees and contractors who travel on company business. AcmeSync is a fictional company; this document is sample data.

## 1. Approval
Trips must be approved by your manager before you book anything. Trips longer than 5 working days, and all international trips, also need approval from the finance team.

## 2. Flights
Book economy class for flights shorter than 6 hours. Premium economy is allowed for flights of 6 hours or longer. Business class is never reimbursed unless the Chief Financial Officer approves it in writing before the trip.

## 3. Hotels
The nightly hotel limit, before taxes, depends on the city:
- Tier 1 cities (London, New York, Tokyo, Singapore): 220 USD per night.
- Tier 2 cities (Berlin, Toronto, Kuala Lumpur, Madrid): 160 USD per night.
- All other cities: 120 USD per night.

## 4. Meals (per diem)
The daily meal allowance depends on the city:
- Tier 1 cities: 75 USD per day.
- Tier 2 cities: 60 USD per day.
- All other cities: 45 USD per day.

On the first and the last day of a trip, the meal allowance is 75% of the daily amount. Alcohol is not reimbursed.

## 5. Ground transport
Use public transport where it is practical, or a taxi when it is not. If you drive your own car, mileage is reimbursed at 0.62 USD per mile. Parking and road tolls are reimbursed with receipts.

## 6. Submitting expenses
Submit your expenses in the Expenses app within 30 days of returning from the trip. Every expense over 25 USD needs a receipt. Expenses submitted more than 60 days after the trip are not reimbursed.

## 7. Currency
Expenses paid in another currency are converted at the exchange rate of the day of purchase, as shown on your card statement.
